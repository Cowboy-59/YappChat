**[WHAT'S INSIDE]**

**Module 1: Employee or Contractor? (Getting This Right the First Time)**
The single most important decision in the hiring process, and the one most new employers get wrong. By the end of this module, you will know exactly which structure fits your situation and why, so you don't accidentally misclassify your hire and create a tax problem later.

**Module 2: Writing a Job Post That Attracts the Right Person**
Most small business job posts either attract zero applicants or attract fifty wrong ones. This module teaches you to write a post that does one thing: bring in the specific person you need. By the end, you will have a draft job post ready to publish.

**Module 3: The Interview Process for Non-Interviewers**
You have probably been interviewed many times but probably haven't run many interviews yourself. This module gives you a structured interview process, a question bank, and a simple scoring method so you stop relying on gut feeling alone.

**Module 4: The Paperwork and Onboarding Checklist**
The part everyone dreads. This module walks through every document you need, why you need it, and where to get it. By the end, you will have a complete onboarding packet ready before your new hire's first day.

**Module 5: The 30-Day Plan (Setting Your New Hire Up to Succeed)**
Most hiring mistakes happen in the first 30 days, not in the interview. This module shows you how to set clear expectations, check in without micromanaging, and know early whether the hire is working.

**Module 6: What to Do When It's Not Working**
No one wants to think about this, but every employer eventually faces it. This module covers performance conversations, documentation, and the right way to end a working relationship if you need to, without legal exposure or unnecessary conflict.

**[FAQ]**

**How long will this course take?**
The six modules total about four hours of video content. Most students complete one module per week alongside running their business, finishing in six weeks. The course is self-paced with no deadlines, so if life gets busy you can pick up where you left off.

**I'm not sure I can afford an employee right now. Is this still for me?**
Module 1 covers this directly. Before you write a job post, you'll work through whether a hire makes financial sense at your current revenue, and what structure (part-time, full-time, or project-based contractor) fits your budget. Some students go through Module 1 and decide to wait six months; they still find that module worth the course price.

**What if I hire the wrong person?**
Module 6 is built for exactly this situation. And the course's overall goal is to reduce the chance of a wrong hire through a better process. The structured interview process and 30-day plan in Modules 3 and 5 are specifically designed to surface fit issues early.

**I'm worried about the legal side. Is this legal advice?**
This course is not legal advice and does not replace an employment attorney for complex situations. What it does is ensure you know the standard requirements, have the standard documentation, and avoid the most common compliance mistakes. For anything specific to your state or situation, consulting a local HR professional or employment attorney is always the right call.

**Does this work for contractors and freelancers, not just employees?**
Yes. Module 1 covers the contractor vs. employee distinction directly, and the material on onboarding, expectations, and performance applies to both relationships.

**What is the refund policy?**
If you complete Modules 1 and 2 and are not satisfied with the course, contact us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. No hoops to jump through.

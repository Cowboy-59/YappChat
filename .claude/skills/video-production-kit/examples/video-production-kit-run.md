# Worked Example: Video Production Kit

Sample brand (for illustration only): Tobias Mensah is a freelance finance consultant who teaches a self-paced course, "Freelance Finance 101," to independent professionals who want to manage cash flow, set rates, and handle quarterly taxes without an accountant. His audience is 28-42-year-old freelancers in creative and tech fields. He records from a home office with a Logitech webcam and a Blue Yeti USB mic, and prefers to be on camera for connection but wants a faceless option for complex spreadsheet lessons.

---

## Inputs provided

- **Module:** Module 4, "Your First Quarterly Tax Estimate"
- **Module description:** This module walks students through calculating their first quarterly estimated tax payment using a simple four-step formula. It covers gross income, deductible expenses, taxable income, and the 25-30% rule for setting aside taxes.
- **Key talking points:** Why freelancers owe quarterly taxes; the safe-harbor rule (pay 90% of this year's or 100% of last year's tax); four-step formula; how to actually pay (IRS Direct Pay website); mistakes to avoid.
- **Recording style:** Talking head for the opening and closing; screencast for the IRS Direct Pay walkthrough; slides for the formula steps.
- **Recording environment:** Home office.
- **Concerns:** Spreadsheet screen is small and hard to see; does not want to show real tax info on screen.

---

## Output

### Scene Map

| Scene | Type | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Opening: why this matters | Talking head | 1.5 min | Personal, high energy; address the fear directly |
| 2. The safe-harbor rule | Slides with voiceover | 2 min | One slide per concept; simple numbers |
| 3. The four-step formula | Slides with voiceover | 3 min | One slide per step; show a fictional example with clean numbers |
| 4. Formula demo with sample numbers | Screencast (spreadsheet) | 4 min | Use a fictional example spreadsheet with dummy data |
| 5. IRS Direct Pay walkthrough | Screencast (browser) | 3 min | Walk through the site with a clean browser (no saved passwords visible) |
| 6. Mistakes to avoid | Talking head | 2 min | Three bullet points, conversational delivery |
| 7. Recap and next step | Talking head | 1 min | End with a clear action: complete the worksheet in the module resources |

---

### Shot List

**TALKING HEAD SCENES (record all at once)**

- Scene 1: Medium shot, Tobias directly to camera. Visible from chest up. Open with: "If you have ever received a surprise tax bill in April, this module is the one that fixes that." Duration: ~90 seconds.
- Scene 6: Same framing. Three mistakes, conversational. No notes needed; memorize bullet points before recording.
- Scene 7: Same framing. Short. End on a warm smile and clear instruction: "Download the worksheet and complete it for your actual numbers."

**SCREENCAST SCENES (record in a separate session)**

- Scene 4: Open a spreadsheet with a fictional example (freelancer named "Alex Chen," dummy income of $7,400/month). Increase zoom to 150% before recording. Walk through the four-step formula by typing values into the cells while narrating each step.
- Scene 5: Open a clean Chrome window in incognito mode (no saved form data or passwords visible). Navigate to irs.gov/payments (show this URL on screen briefly). Walk through the Direct Pay flow, stopping before any actual payment page. Note on screen: "We will not actually submit a payment in this demo."

**SLIDES SCENES (produce slides first, record voiceover last)**

- Scene 2: Two slides. Slide 1: "The Safe-Harbor Rule" with a simple two-option comparison. Slide 2: The exact definition and the threshold number (note: always verify current IRS numbers at the time of recording; tax rules change).
- Scene 3: Four slides, one per formula step. Use large text and simple arithmetic. Fictional example: Alex Chen, $7,400 gross, $1,200 deductions, $6,200 taxable, $1,550 set aside at 25%.

### Lighting and Audio Setup Checklist (Home Office)

**Light:**
- [ ] Position desk so Tobias faces the window (natural light as key light). If no window or afternoon light is weak, use the ring light at 45 degrees, slightly above eye level.
- [ ] Place a white foam board or piece of white card stock to the right of Tobias to fill in shadow on the off-camera side.
- [ ] Check the background: no windows behind Tobias visible in frame (backlight). A clean shelf or neutral wall is ideal.
- [ ] Test by recording 5 seconds and watching the playback: no harsh shadows under eyes, face evenly lit.

**Audio:**
- [ ] Blue Yeti: set to Cardioid mode. Position 7-8 inches from mouth, slightly below chin, angled up. This reduces desk and breath noise.
- [ ] Record 15 seconds of silence before content. Listen for fan, HVAC, or street noise. If present, address before recording.
- [ ] Target: audio peaks between -12dB and -6dB. Check in the recording software's meter.
- [ ] Phone: Do Not Disturb, face down, in another room if possible.

### Faceless Option: Module 4

For this module, the screencast scenes already require no on-camera presence. The talking-head scenes (1, 6, 7) can be replaced with the following approach:

**Opening (Scene 1 replacement):** Slide with large, direct text: "Most freelancers only find out they owe quarterly taxes the first time they miss one." Voiceover delivers the opening lines. Cut to the formula content immediately.

**Mistakes (Scene 6 replacement):** Three slides, one per mistake. Title each slide "Mistake 1:", "Mistake 2:", "Mistake 3:" with a clean one-line description. Voiceover narrates. No on-camera required.

**Closing (Scene 7 replacement):** Final slide: "Your action step: Download the worksheet in the module resources and complete it with your actual numbers." Voiceover says the line. End card appears.

Tone note: the voiceover for this module should stay warm and direct, not clinical. Tobias's voice should sound like a friend who knows taxes, not a government form.

# Worked Example: Student Success Engine

Sample brand (for illustration only): Kwame Asante teaches a $297 self-paced course called "Pitch Like a Founder" to early-stage entrepreneurs who want to pitch investors, accelerators, or potential co-founders. The course has 5 modules and takes about 6 hours to complete. There is a Circle community. His students tend to drop off after Module 2 because Module 3 (building the financial slide) is the most technically demanding and scary part. He does not currently have a Klaviyo connection.

## Drop-Off Map and Nudge Emails

**Identified drop-off points:**

1. **Before starting (Day 5-10 with no login):** Student bought with intent but hasn't logged in.
2. **After Module 2 (the most common drop-off point):** Module 3 covers financial slides and students who don't have a finance background get stuck or intimidated.
3. **At 80% (Module 4 complete, Module 5 not started):** Proximity effect; the student has enough to pitch and stops short of finishing.

You just finished Module 2. Nice work.

Before you jump into Module 3, I want to say something directly: this is the module most students find most challenging.

It's not because the content is complex, it's because financial slides tend to trigger the "I'm not a numbers person" feeling in a lot of founders. That feeling is normal and it doesn't mean you're doing it wrong.

Module 3 assumes no accounting background. Everything is explained with a fictional startup example before you work through your own numbers. Give yourself a two-hour block rather than 30 minutes, and if something doesn't click the first time, the worksheet has a worked example you can compare against.

## Community Prompts (7 prompts for Circle community)

1. **Introduction prompt:** "Introduce yourself in two sentences: your startup or project in one sentence, and what you want to be able to say to an investor or accelerator in the next 90 days."

2. **Progress check-in:** "Which module are you on this week? Drop a quick update and tell us one thing that has clicked so far."

3. **Skill application:** "Module 2 covers the investor's decision framework. Try this: in 2 sentences, explain your startup using the 'here's the problem, here's why we solve it better' structure. Share it here for feedback."

4. **Specific challenge:** "What's the hardest part of your pitch right now? One sentence. Let's see if the group can help."

5. **Peer feedback request:** "If you have a draft slide you'd like feedback on, post an image here. Keep feedback specific: one thing that works, one thing to sharpen."

6. **Celebration post:** "Who has a win to share this week? A pitch meeting scheduled, a module completed, feedback received? Post it. We're here for it."

7. **Reflection question:** "Now that you're deeper into the course: what was the one thing about investor pitching you believed before taking this course that turned out to be wrong or incomplete?"
